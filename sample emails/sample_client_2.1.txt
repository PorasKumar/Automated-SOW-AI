SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Project: AI-Powered Customer Support & Ticket Routing System
Client: Zenith Retail Brands

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:
1. Omnichannel Ingestion:
   - Ingest customer support queries from Email, Web Chat, and WhatsApp Webhooks.
   - Parse incoming attachments (PDF invoices, product images).

2. Automated Classification & Routing:
   - Categorize support tickets: Returns, Order Tracking, Product Inquiries, Fraud Alerts.
   - Use vector similarity search (PGVector) against internal knowledge base for auto-replies.
   - High-priority tickets (Fraud/Escalations) must bypass auto-reply and ping Slack #support-urgent channel.

3. Technical Stack Expectations:
   - Backend: Python / FastAPI
   - Orchestration: LangGraph or LangChain
   - Vector Store: PostgreSQL with pgvector extension
   - Worker Engine: Redis Queue (RQ) for async background jobs
